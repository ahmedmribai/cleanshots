## Full description (max 4000)
Your gallery is full of things you don't need: old screenshots, five copies of the same shot, blurry photos and videos that eat gigabytes. CleanShots finds them in seconds and lets you clear them in a few taps, without anything leaving your phone.

**Smart Clean in one tap**
CleanShots groups duplicate and near-duplicate photos, keeps the best one of each set (highest resolution, sharpest) and clears the rest in a single tap. Want a different copy? Long-press to compare and keep it instead.

**Swipe review**
Fly through screenshots and blurry photos one card at a time: swipe left to clean, right to keep. Undo any decision. Nothing is removed until you confirm.

**Find what takes the most space**
• Screenshots, collected from every app
• Duplicate and near-duplicate photos
• Blurry, out-of-focus shots (dark night photos are left alone)
• Large videos, biggest first

**Safe by design**
• Everything goes to the Android Trash first, so you can undo for 30 days
• Android asks for your confirmation before anything is removed
• Undo straight from the app

**Private by design**
• All analysis happens on your phone
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no uploads
• Works with only the photos you choose to share

**Free to try**
Clean your first 20 items free. Upgrade to CleanShots Pro (weekly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase) for unlimited cleaning.

Weekly scan reminders tell you when there is space to free up. Available in English, Arabic, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish and Turkish.

## Photo and video permissions declaration
Core purpose: the app's main function is finding and removing unwanted photos and videos from the
user's media library (screenshots, duplicates, blurry photos, large videos). This requires broad
read access to images and videos; the Android photo picker cannot provide it because the app
must scan the whole library to find duplicates. Partial access (selected photos) is supported.
