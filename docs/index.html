CleanShots Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

CleanShots ("the app") helps you find and remove screenshots, duplicate photos and blurry photos on your Android device. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Short version

Your photos never leave your device. CleanShots has no account system, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising.

Photos and media

The app asks for permission to read your photos so it can analyse them on your device. It creates a small thumbnail of each photo in memory to compute a numeric fingerprint (used to spot duplicates) and a sharpness score (used to spot blurry photos). These results, together with each photo's file name, size, dimensions and folder, are stored in a private database inside the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded, shared or sold.

When you choose to delete photos, the app asks Android to move them to the system Trash. Android shows its own confirmation dialog, and nothing is removed unless you approve it. Items in the Trash can be restored from within the app or by Android until Android empties the Trash (typically after 30 days).

Purchases

Premium features are sold through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details. The app stores on your device only whether you currently have an active purchase and how many free deletions you have used.

Data we collect

None. The developer does not receive, collect or store any personal data from the app.

Third parties

The only third-party service the app talks to is Google Play (for purchases). Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

You can revoke the photo permission at any time in Android Settings, and uninstalling the app removes all data it stored.
